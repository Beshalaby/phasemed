Raw scans. Useful data.

Phasmed turns CT and other DICOM studies into structured patient models with measurable anatomy, traceable evidence, and data AI can use.

Open workstation ↗ How it works ↓

From raw scans to structured data. In three steps.

  1. 01

    Import

    Load CT and other DICOM studies from a folder or gateway. Phasmed validates each series and records where it came from.

  2. 02

    Build

    Convert volumes and segmentations into labeled 3D objects with coordinates, measurements, and links to the source images.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Measure relationships and change across studies, then review, query, or export the results for people and AI.

Patient models. Useful to AI and doctors.

AI can understand structured anatomy, measurements, relationships, and source evidence instead of piecing together raw images and scattered context. Doctors can ask direct questions in the viewer, inspect the evidence, compare studies, and analyze further.

One model. Five ways in.

  • 01Review

    Slices stay linked to objects and coordinates.

  • 02Change

    Priors, overlays and differences, measured.

  • 03Query

    Deterministic answers, for clinicians and AI alike.

  • 04Hologram

    A four-view render with local voice control.

  • 05Export

    JSON and JSON-LD for whatever comes next.

Patient models in. Useful models out.

Use the model to review anatomy, compare follow-ups, query evidence and export structured data. Train and validate AI on measured geometry and provenance instead of raw pixels, with every result linked back to its source.
