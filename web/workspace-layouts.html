Phasmed · workspace

Workspace layout studies

Six arrangements of the same workstation, drawn to the proportions the app actually ships (topbar 48px, commandbar 42px, rails 238px and 306px). The numbers under each are the real usable viewport at the selected window size, so the trade-off is a measurement rather than a feeling.

Schematics are proportional, not pixel-exact renders. Measurements taken from web/styles.css: .topbar 48px, .commandbar 42px, .workstation grid-template-columns: 238px minmax(520px, 1fr) 306px, and the 1280px breakpoint that narrows the rails to 205px and 270px. Viewport area is the canvas left for the model after chrome, rails and strips.
